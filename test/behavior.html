Toasts

A box in a stack pinned to a corner. The message types in without the box moving, and the bottom border doubles as the countdown: it erodes a cell at a time until it is gone and the toast goes with it.

Modals

A native <dialog> that is also a box, so the focus trap, Escape and the top layer are the browser’s work and the border is the grid layer’s. The dim behind it is a run of block glyphs painted in the background colour, not a translucent black.
